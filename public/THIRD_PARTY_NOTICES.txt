Cooked 3D models (models/char-line-soldier, models/prop-echo-well)

Reconstructed from original, locally generated concepts with Microsoft
TRELLIS-image-large (https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS, model revision
25e0d31ffbebe4b5a97464dd851910efc3002d96). TRELLIS code and weights are
MIT-licensed, but the textured export these models went through depends on
components restricted to research and evaluation (the Gaussian export path and
diffoctreerast). No commercial clearance has been established for this output.
Provenance, recipes and approvals are kept with the game source in scripts/models.
